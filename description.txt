[h1]Viewpoint Gael Compatibility[/h1]
First-person ADS compatibility for GaelGunStore_B42 with Viewpoint and Project Viewpoint ADS. The patch aligns Gael firearms and supported optics, and includes an in-game slider calibrator with weapon search, live adjustments, Save/Copy/Reset controls, and an "Añadir Retícula" selector for base sights, reflex reticles, or 2x/4x/8x scope mode. Choices are saved per weapon and optic.

[b]Calibration status:[/b] 157 weapons have manually saved iron-sight profiles. Two more were verified in game using their default alignment. Selected optics also have manual profiles. Other weapon and optic combinations may still need visual calibration.

[h1]Experimental options — enabled by default for testing[/h1]
[list]
[*] Iron-sight focus: crouching narrows the calibrated FOV. The mouse wheel or your Project Viewpoint ADS zoom keys add focus with a smooth 0.4-second zoom. An optional sandbox setting adds the vanilla search-mode blur around the view only during manual focus. If zoom is attempted before breath is ready, a brief red X and "Espera..." appear beside the sight instead of the bar. A translucent bar appears only during manual focus. On activation it moves smoothly from yellow toward green when breath is plentiful, then through orange to translucent red as breath runs out; it shows breath capacity: 5 seconds plus 1 second per Fitness level, reduced by Endurance exertion. While stationary, focus reduces temporary sight movement by 70%. Firing or running out of breath ends focus. Sprinting drains 1 second of breath per real second; normal running drains 0.5 seconds. Breath recharges after a 0.4-second cooldown at 0.25 seconds of breath per real second, increased by 10% per Fitness level; at least 25% capacity must recover before focus can be used again.
[*] Extended firearm range: effective range increases by 50%. Hits on characters beyond the original range deal 30% less damage.
[*] Dynamic aiming: ADS sway responds to weapon weight and length, Strength, and Endurance exertion. Each shot recenters the sway; the character realigns smoothly after a random 3–5 seconds. Exertion also moves the sight on X/Y/Z and can briefly lower the arms. Walking adds smooth Y movement and progressive Roll.
[*] Arm muscle fatigue: mild arm stiffness adds 150% to sway and shot recoil; moderate or greater stiffness adds 300%. It also introduces small irregular aim corrections. This uses arm muscle stiffness separately from the Endurance moodle.
[*] Shot recoil: the patch and Project Viewpoint ADS recoil amplitudes are reduced by 60% for Gael firearms. Any attached grip adds another 40% recoil reduction to both effects (24% of the original recoil remains); handguards do not count, and multiple grips do not stack. Pitch and Yaw move independently, each with a 50/50 chance of either direction. Weapon weight, pistol ammunition, and exertion affect the angles. Shots 1–3 of a continuous sequence use 50% recoil, shots 4–5 use 75%, and later shots use full recoil. Only in Auto mode, from shot 21 onward both axes gain 3 degrees, keep their direction, and receive a new random Pitch/Yaw impulse around that held position on every further shot. Each bullet advances part of a 4–5-shot path toward a random different corner, avoiding the previous corner; the movement is smoothed between shots. Releasing the trigger after 26 or more Auto shots makes the sight trace a brief irregular circular motion while the held recoil returns to center over 0.4 seconds before Fitness bonuses. A pause longer than one second, changing weapons, or changing fire mode resets the sequence. In Auto mode, Strength further reduces each shot from 1/8 at level 0 to 1/28.8 at level 10. From shot 6, Fitness levels 5–10 speed up the return to center by 10% per level above 4; from shot 26, the total bonus is 15% per level above 4, including the release regrip. Fitness levels 0–4 give no recovery bonus. Ordinary recoil settles over a random 0.3–0.5 seconds, or 0.4–0.7 seconds for heavier weapons; after shot 20 in Auto mode it holds briefly and settles over 1.15 seconds, shortening to 1.00 second from shot 27 onward.
[*] Cycling a firearm bolt or slide moves sight Pitch smoothly up or down during the rack action.
[/list]
These options can be changed in Sandbox Settings. Dynamic aiming does not overwrite saved weapon calibrations.

[h1]Optics and requirements[/h1]
Supported Gael 1x sights use reflex mode; magnified 2x–8x optics use scope mode on supported rifles.

Requires Viewpoint, GaelGunStore_B42, and ProjectViewpointADS. For Project Zomboid Build 42.21. This Workshop package contains one mod: ViewpointGaelCompat. Restart the game after installing or updating.
